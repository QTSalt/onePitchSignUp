I am voluntarily choosing to participate in the Event and freely accept and assume all such risks.

In consideration of being permitted to participate, I, on behalf of myself and my heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns, release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue Quinntessential Softball and its organizers, directors, officers, volunteers, and staff; the owners and operators of Lower Woodlands Ballfield; tournament umpires and officials; and each of their respective employees and agents (collectively, the "Released Parties") from any and all liability, claims, demands, actions, and causes of action arising out of or related to any loss, damage, or injury, including death, that may be sustained while participating in the Event, whether caused by the negligence of the Released Parties or otherwise, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any loss, liability, damage, or cost they may incur due to my presence at or participation in the Event, whether caused by my own negligence or otherwise.

I authorize the Event's medical staff or designated first responders to render first aid and, if necessary, to arrange for further medical treatment, including transportation to a medical facility, in the event of injury or illness. I understand I am responsible for any costs associated with such treatment.

I agree to abide by all tournament rules, including the official USA Softball rulebook where applicable, and understand that failure to do so may result in removal from the Event without refund.

If any provision of this agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect. This agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which the Event is held.

9. Acknowledgment

I confirm that I have read this agreement, understand its terms, understand that I am giving up substantial legal rights, and sign it voluntarily.
