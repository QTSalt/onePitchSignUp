Charity Swing-Off Waiver

Assumption of Risk, Waiver of Liability, and Indemnification Agreement — Fastpitch Pitching

Event: The Charity Swing-Off
Presented by: Quinntessential Softball
When: Immediately following the One Pitch-No Bitch tournament — September 26, 2026
Location: Lower Woodlands Ballfield — Field 3 & Field 6
Format: Batter faces 5 pitches from a fastpitch softball pitcher
⚠ This event involves live fastpitch pitching Fastpitch pitching travels significantly faster and from a shorter effective reaction distance than the slowpitch pitching used in the main tournament. This materially increases the risk of being struck by a pitched ball. Please read the risks below carefully before signing.

1. Acknowledgment of Risk

I acknowledge that the Charity Swing-Off involves batting against live pitches thrown by fastpitch softball pitchers at competitive speeds and close range. I understand that fastpitch pitching differs significantly from the slowpitch pitching used elsewhere in the tournament and carries a materially higher risk of injury, including but not limited to: being struck by a pitched ball at high velocity, which can cause bruising, fracture, concussion, dental or facial injury, or other serious harm; reduced reaction time to avoid an inside pitch, errant throw, or hit ball; injury from swinging at high-speed pitches, including muscle strains and loss of bat control; and, in rare cases, catastrophic injury or death. I understand these risks are materially greater than those typically associated with recreational slowpitch softball, and I voluntarily and knowingly assume them.

2. Voluntary Participation

I am voluntarily choosing to participate in the Charity Swing-Off and freely accept and assume all such risks. I understand I may decline to participate in this event at any time without affecting my participation in the main tournament.

3. Release of Liability

In consideration of being permitted to participate, I, on behalf of myself and my heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns, release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue Quinntessential Softball and its organizers, directors, officers, volunteers, and staff; participating fastpitch pitchers; the owners and operators of Lower Woodlands Ballfield; tournament umpires and officials; and each of their respective employees and agents (collectively, the "Released Parties") from any and all liability, claims, demands, actions, and causes of action arising out of or related to any loss, damage, or injury, including death, that may be sustained while participating in the Charity Swing-Off, whether caused by the negligence of the Released Parties or otherwise, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

4. Indemnification

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any loss, liability, damage, or cost they may incur due to my presence at or participation in the Charity Swing-Off, whether caused by my own negligence or otherwise.

5. Medical Treatment Authorization

I authorize the Event's medical staff or designated first responders to render first aid and, if necessary, to arrange for further medical treatment, including transportation to a medical facility, in the event of injury or illness. I understand I am responsible for any costs associated with such treatment.

6. Donation Acknowledgment

I understand that any amount I pledge in connection with the Charity Swing-Off, along with any matched amount based on my swing results, represents a charitable contribution and is non-refundable. I understand the donation recipient will be announced by the organizer.

7. Photo / Video Release

I grant Quinntessential Softball permission to photograph and/or video record me during the Charity Swing-Off and to use such images for promotional purposes, without compensation.

8. Severability & Governing Law

If any provision of this agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect. This agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which the Event is held.

9. Acknowledgment

I confirm that I have read this agreement, understand its terms — including that fastpitch pitching carries materially higher risk than slowpitch — understand that I am giving up substantial legal rights, and sign it voluntarily.
